Carfolio Support

Let’s get you moving again.

Start with the steps below. If the issue remains, open a support request without including sensitive vehicle or payment information.

Premium did not unlock

  1. Confirm the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account that started the subscription.
  2. Open the Carfolio paywall and choose Restore Purchases.
  3. Check the subscription status in iOS Settings under your Apple Account.
  4. If Apple reports a pending purchase, wait for approval or payment resolution and try again.

A vehicle lookup failed

  • Israel: enter a 7–8 digit registration plate.
  • United States: enter a complete 17-character VIN.
  • Netherlands: enter the six-character Dutch registration plate; RDW lookup is plate-based.
  • Check the internet connection and retry. A public provider may be temporarily unavailable or may not hold a record for that identifier.

Damage was not detected

Use even lighting, keep the lens clean, hold the phone steady, and frame the damaged panel without excessive background. Reviewable low-confidence signals can still be accepted by the user before appraisal. A missed detection does not prove that the vehicle is undamaged.

Delete local information

Delete a saved vehicle from My Car and remove appraisals or detection attempts from appraisal history. Removing the app deletes its local data container subject to Apple's device and backup behavior.

GitHub support requests are public. Never post a full VIN, registration plate, damage photo, address, payment detail, or other personal information.
