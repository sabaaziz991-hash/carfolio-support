Free and Premium access

Carvetted is free to download. The free experience presents one market-appropriate sample across Buy, Research, Sell, Appraise, and My Car. The samples are demonstrations and are not live lookups for the user's vehicle.

Carvetted Premium unlocks live supported lookups, evidence-based research, selling tools, saved vehicles and recall monitoring, the damage camera, preliminary estimates, and saved appraisal history.

Annual subscription and trial

  • Premium is an auto-renewable annual subscription.
  • Apple displays the price for the active storefront before purchase.
  • A seven-day free trial is available only when Apple determines the Apple Account is eligible.
  • After an eligible trial, the annual subscription is charged unless canceled before the trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Apple manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and refund eligibility.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting Carvetted does not cancel a subscription.

Estimates and vehicle information

Vehicle identity fields, public records, recall indicators, market values, depreciation, damage severity, repair times, parts, paint, labor, recommendations, and scores may be incomplete, delayed, modeled, or unavailable. They are informational estimates—not guaranteed values, ownership or legal-history reports, safety inspections, mechanical diagnoses, professional appraisals, or repair quotes.

Always confirm the exact vehicle, title and ownership history, current recall status, physical and mechanical condition, and repair needs with the relevant authority or qualified professional before buying, selling, driving, insuring, or repairing a vehicle.

Acceptable use

Use Carvetted only for lawful vehicle research and ownership tasks. Do not use it to harass another person, build an unauthorized identity profile, evade legal restrictions, overwhelm a public data provider, or misrepresent an estimate as a certified report.

Availability

Data sources, fields, coverage, images, and model behavior vary across Israel, the United States, and the Netherlands and may change without notice. Carvetted may correct, suspend, or remove a source or feature when required for reliability, safety, or provider compliance.
