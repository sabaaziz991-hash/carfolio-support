Privacy Policy

Your vehicle data stays under your control.

Carvetted uses public vehicle-data services only when you request a lookup. Damage-photo analysis runs on your device.

Effective August 31, 2026

Summary

  • Carvetted does not include advertising SDKs, sell personal information, or track you across apps and websites.
  • Damage photos are analyzed on the device. They are stored by Carvetted only when you save a detection or appraisal.
  • A plate, VIN, or other vehicle query is sent to the selected public data provider only when that lookup is requested.
  • Saved vehicles, appraisals, value history, sale drafts, and notes are stored locally on your device.
  • Apple processes subscriptions and payment information. Carvetted receives entitlement status, not your card or bank details.

Information you provide

You may enter a registration plate, VIN or chassis number, mileage, ownership details, asking price, comparable values, repair inputs, and notes. You may also take or select vehicle photos. Carvetted uses this information to provide the feature you selected.

On-device information

Carvetted stores saved vehicles, recall checks, value-history points, sale drafts, detection attempts, appraisals, and saved photos in the app's local storage. Free sample mode does not create live registry lookups or add records to your garage.

The damage models run on the device. Carvetted does not upload a damage photo to its own server. Representative vehicle images may be fetched from a public image source using make and model search terms.

Public data providers

When you request a live lookup, Carvetted may send the minimum vehicle query required to the relevant source:

  • Israel: Israeli government open-data services and supported public price or safety sources.
  • United States: NHTSA, FuelEconomy.gov/EPA, and supported government valuation sources.
  • Netherlands: RDW public vehicle datasets.
  • Vehicle images: Wikimedia Commons searches based on available make and model information.

These providers receive the network information normally included in an internet request and the query needed for the lookup. Their own privacy policies and retention practices apply. Availability and returned fields vary by market and vehicle.

Subscriptions

Apple handles purchase, trial eligibility, renewal, cancellation, refund, and payment processing. Carvetted checks StoreKit for a verified subscription entitlement so it can unlock Premium. Carvetted does not receive your full payment details.

Notifications and background refresh

If you allow notifications, Carvetted may notify you about available recall changes for saved vehicles. Background refresh is used only for supported recall monitoring. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in Carvetted or remove the app. Deleting a saved vehicle removes its garage data as described in the app; separately saved appraisal records can be deleted from appraisal history. Removing the app deletes its local container subject to Apple's device and backup behavior.

Children

Carvetted is a general vehicle utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children through an account system because Carvetted does not provide user accounts.

Security and limits

Carvetted uses encrypted HTTPS connections where supported by the public provider. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Avoid entering personal information in free-text notes and do not publish full plates, VINs, payment details, addresses, or photos in public support requests.

Changes

This policy may change when Carvetted's features or providers change. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.

Questions

Use the Carvetted support page to report a privacy or product issue without including a full VIN, plate, payment detail, address, or damage photo.
